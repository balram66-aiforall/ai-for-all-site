# Cave material provenance

Original procedural basalt-like albedo, OpenGL normal and roughness maps created locally for this prototype. Seed 66. No third-party image incorporated.

An ambientCG Rock030 download was investigated (CC0) but both the ZIP and surface-preview hosts failed; none of those files are included.

Artifactory-generated environment visuals are pending Midway authentication.

`cave-desktop.webp` and `cave-mobile.webp` are still renders captured from the original local Three.js environment, not Artifactory generations.
