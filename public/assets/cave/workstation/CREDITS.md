# Workstation artwork provenance

Local bAIram prototype, 2026-10-11. Nothing uploaded or published.

- `original.webp`: lossless copy of the decoded Artifactory-generated `precision-deck.jpg`, approved by Balram before this build. 1344 x 768.
- `clean-plate.webp`: Artifactory `edit_image` replacements remove the two upper arms. Only masked arm regions (3.77% of pixels) incorporate the generated replacement rock; all pixels elsewhere, including the desk and seated human, remain identical to the original decoded image.
- `arm-left.webp`, `arm-right.webp`: Artifactory `REMOVE_BACKGROUND` mattes, locally cleaned and feathered, over original source RGB pixels. Full-frame 1344 x 768 RGBA. Arms are animated as layers, not live mechanical/agent activity.
- `suit.webp`: earlier Artifactory `suit.jpg` concept, cropped to 832 x 1180 with a four-pixel plinth pseudo-text strip removed. No claim that the helmet/chest identity is final.

Sources, prompts, hashes and rejected intermediate generations: `frontier-aimplify/work/builder/2026-10-11-bairam-workstation/assets-prep/` in the local Aki workspace. Artifactory official installed MCP callbacks were used for generation/editing; no artifact-upload or sharing operation was used.

Original source images remain unchanged. No Batman logo, film still, soundtrack or third-party character artwork is incorporated. The seated figure is an illustrative stand-in, not a verified portrait. Code-drawn bats and screen traces are local choreographed animation, not Artifactory-generated video.

The parent cave folder contains older procedural materials and 3D-rendered stills, which are retained for the superseded prototype and are not used by the current workstation.
